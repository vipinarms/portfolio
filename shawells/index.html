01 / Our material story

Colour is only the beginning.

AM/NS UltraShine is a premium pre-painted profile sheet range from AM/NS India, formerly known as ISC Ultrashine XL. Its advanced coating system combines a strong steel base with protection that is made for the long haul.

From a family home to a high-traffic industrial building, Shawells helps you choose the right profile, finish and colour for the job.

01

Roofing

Trafford, E Tile, S Tile and standing-seam profiles for weatherproof, expressive rooflines.

02

Cladding

Liner, C Clad, S Clad and P Clad options for durable walls and considered details.

03

Profiles

Superior profile design for better drainage, less pooling and confident installation.
